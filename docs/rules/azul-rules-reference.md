# Azul — Rules Reference

This is an original plain-language summary of the base game rules, written for Board Game Master.
It does not reproduce the official rulebook by Plan B Games / Next Move Games. It covers the base
game for 2 to 4 players, including the gray-side variant, and gives no strategy advice.

## Overview of the game

Azul is a tile-drafting game for 2 to 4 players. Each player is a tile artist decorating a palace
wall. Players take turns picking colored tiles from shared Factory displays, arrange them on the
pattern lines of their own player board, and then move completed lines onto their wall to score
points. The game is played over several rounds.

## Object of the game and how to win

The goal is to have the most points when the game ends. You score points each time you move a
tile onto your wall, lose points for tiles in your floor line, and gain bonus points at the end of
the game. The player with the most points wins.

## Components

The game contains 100 tiles in 5 colors (20 of each color), a bag for the tiles, 9 Factory
displays, 4 player boards, 4 scoring markers and 1 starting player marker. Each player board has
a score track, 5 pattern lines on the left, a 5-by-5 wall on the right, and a floor line along
the bottom.

## Setup

1. Give each player a player board. Everyone uses the side with the colored wall (see Variant:
   the gray side of the board for the other side). All players must use the same side.
2. Each player puts a scoring marker on the 0 space of their score track.
3. Place Factory displays in a circle in the middle of the table: 5 displays with 2 players,
   7 with 3 players, or 9 with 4 players.
4. Put all 100 tiles into the bag.
5. Choose a starting player (the rulebook suggests the player who most recently visited
   Portugal). That player takes the starting player marker and fills every Factory display with
   exactly 4 tiles drawn at random from the bag.

Return any unused boards, scoring markers and Factory displays to the box.

## Structure of a round

Each round has three phases, played in this order:

- A. Factory offer: players take turns picking tiles and placing them on their pattern lines.
- B. Wall-tiling: players move tiles from completed pattern lines onto their wall and score.
- C. Preparing the next round: the Factory displays are refilled, unless the game has ended.

## Your turn: picking tiles

At the start of the Factory offer phase, the player with the starting player marker puts it in
the center of the table and takes the first turn. Play then goes clockwise.

On your turn you must pick tiles in one of two ways:

- Take every tile of one color from one Factory display, then push the other tiles from that
  display into the center of the table.
- Or take every tile of one color from the center of the table. If you are the first player
  this round to take tiles from the center, you also take the starting player marker and put
  it on the leftmost free space of your floor line.

After picking, you must place the tiles you took (see Placing tiles on your pattern lines).

## Placing tiles on your pattern lines

Put all the tiles you picked into one of your 5 pattern lines. The first line holds 1 tile, the
second holds 2, and so on up to 5 tiles in the fifth line.

- Fill a pattern line from right to left, one tile at a time.
- A pattern line can only ever hold tiles of one color. If it already has tiles, you may only
  add tiles of that same color.
- You may not put tiles of a color into a pattern line when the matching row of your wall
  already has a tile of that color.
- A pattern line is complete when all of its spaces are filled.
- Tiles that do not fit in the line you chose go to your floor line.

You may also choose to put all of the tiles you picked straight into your floor line.

## Floor line and penalties

Your floor line holds tiles you picked but could not, or chose not to, place on a pattern line.
Fill it from left to right. Each tile there costs points at the end of the Wall-tiling phase:
you lose the number of points printed above its space (on the colored side: -1, -1, -2, -2, -2,
-3, -3 from left to right). Your score can never go below 0.

If your floor line is full, any further tiles you would put there go into the game box lid
instead.

The starting player marker counts as a tile in your floor line and costs points like one. At the
end of Wall-tiling, you keep it in front of you instead of discarding it, and you start the next
round.

## End of the Factory offer phase

The Factory offer phase ends when there are no tiles left on any Factory display or in the
center of the table. Then the Wall-tiling phase begins.

## Wall-tiling phase

All players can do this phase at the same time.

1. Check your pattern lines from top to bottom. For each complete line, move its rightmost tile
   onto the space of the same color in the matching row of your wall, and score that tile at
   once (see Scoring a tile placed on the wall).
2. Then remove all the other tiles from each line you just used and put them in the game box
   lid. Incomplete pattern lines keep their tiles on your board for the next round.
3. Finally, lose points for each tile in your floor line, then clear your floor line into the
   box lid (keeping the starting player marker in front of you if you had it).

## Scoring a tile placed on the wall

Each tile moved onto your wall scores immediately:

- If it has no tile directly next to it horizontally or vertically, you score 1 point.
- Otherwise, count the unbroken row of tiles it is part of horizontally (including the new tile)
  and score that many points, if that row is 2 or more tiles. Then count the unbroken column it
  is part of vertically (including the new tile) and score that many points, if that column is 2
  or more tiles.

For example, a new tile in a horizontal run of 4 tiles that is also in a vertical run of 3 tiles
scores 4 + 3 = 7 points.

## Preparing the next round

If no player has completed a full horizontal row of 5 tiles on their wall, a new round starts.
The player with the starting player marker fills each Factory display with 4 tiles from the bag.
If the bag runs out, put all the tiles from the game box lid back into the bag and keep filling.
If there are still not enough tiles, start the round anyway with some displays not fully filled.

## How the game ends

The game ends at the end of the Wall-tiling phase of the round in which at least one player
completed at least one horizontal row of 5 tiles on their wall. No further round is played.

## Final scoring and bonuses

When the game ends, each player adds bonus points:

- 2 points for each complete horizontal row of 5 tiles on their wall.
- 7 points for each complete vertical column of 5 tiles on their wall.
- 10 points for each color of which they placed all 5 tiles on their wall.

## Winner and ties

The player with the most points wins. If players are tied, the tied player with more complete
horizontal rows on their wall wins. If they are still tied, they share the victory.

## Variant: the gray side of the board

For a different game, all players use the gray side of their player board. The rules are the same,
except that when you move a tile from a pattern line to your wall, you may put it on any space of
the matching wall row. No color may appear more than once in any row or any column of your wall.

If you cannot move the rightmost tile of a complete pattern line onto your wall because no valid
space is left for it, you must put all the tiles of that pattern line into your floor line.
